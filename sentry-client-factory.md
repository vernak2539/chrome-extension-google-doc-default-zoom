topic: sentry
---

Sentry clients are created through `createSentryClient` in `src/utils/sentry/base.ts`: background handlers `execute-enter.ts`, `get-app-storage-state.ts` and `report-content-error.ts` (which also reports for the content script, `source` tag `content`), `src/utils/migrations.ts`, popup settings, and the popup/tab error boundaries. The content script bundle has no Sentry. `...extraClientOptions` is spread before `beforeSend`, so callers cannot override the redaction; the second parameter type omits `beforeSend`. `redactEventRequest` / `redactUrl` (`src/utils/sentry/redact-event.ts`) rebuild `event.request` as `{ url, headers }`, with the URL reduced to origin plus app segment and the `Referer` header dropped. Options that must apply to every client go after `...extraClientOptions`. `src/utils/sentry/base.test.ts` runs the real client with a dummy `example.invalid` DSN, a recording transport and stubbed `document`/`location`/`chrome`, and needs `vi.mock("@plasmohq/storage", ...)`. `@sentry/core` isn't a direct dependency, so types come from `@sentry/browser`.
